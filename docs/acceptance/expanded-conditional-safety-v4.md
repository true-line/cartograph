## Versioned contract delta

This supplement replaces only the unproved full-budget-fit prerequisite in the planned expanded control with verified conditional safety. It does not rewrite `docs/acceptance/v7-completion-control-v2.md`, its protocol identity, historical expanded test-map entries, or failed receipts.
The single expanded target is request7 / specialist @10 / prompt9 / disclosure6 / result7 under unchanged investigation-v2 limits; planned request5/@8 is superseded, not a second harness.
Frozen V7 remains request4/@7 with its existing entrypoints and byte/hash identities. Runtime AC-0287 evidence supports, but cannot discharge, open AC-0191/T-0191 (#598).

For every admitted invocation, prove the exact production transport representation and protected-field sealing for its saved role, mode, complete parent/history, usage, completion metadata and capacity feedback. Record exact initial prompt size separately.
Before accepting an action or reading source, establish the applicable sound contribution bound; before publishing acquisition, check the exact serialized continuation on the unchanged basis. Unproven/oversized/overflowing candidates fail closed; a bound contradiction is an invariant defect, not ordinary refusal.
Prove Choice and FinishOnly variants, simultaneous bounded feedback and remaining finishing calls/generated-token reserve independently. Wire bytes do not certify unknown tokenizer/provider context capacity; unknowns stay explicit and cannot become a pass.
Capacity refusal preserves incurred model charges and durable refusal identity without inventing acquisition/attempt/citation or tool debits. FinishOnly tool proposals terminate without acquisition or refusal-count inflation. Host-generated partial success is forbidden.
Successful conditional admission proves neither all reachable full-budget prompts fit nor requested coverage is achievable. Refusal-driven/partial/insufficient-evidence outcomes never establish full-obligation acceptance; preserve actual coverage and T3 uncertainty.
