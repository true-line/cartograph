Every export now includes `external.md`. It lists what the ingested sources call or publish to outside themselves. Each web address whose scheme and host are written in the source, and that no declared binding covers, is listed once as outside, with every call site, its method and path where known, its source span, the flows that reach it and the data entities those flows read or write. The entities column does not say that an entity is sent. A target that is not proven inside or outside is listed as unclassified with its reason: a host computed at run time or read from configuration, an SDK or identity-provider client, and a published channel with no recovered subscriber or backing resource. A call that a declared binding places in another ingested repository is counted and not listed. The outside list is a lower bound. Without a declared binding, a call to another ingested repository by its full address is listed as outside; when bindings exist, a call to a host they do not cover is listed as unclassified with the sentence recorded for it. Every row carries the tier and confidence of its fact and never a higher one. A `verified-only` export leaves out InferredWeak rows and states how many; a `best-effort` export marks them. The document cannot be selected in a work packet.
