Every export now includes `product-specification.md`, the first document arranged as the document catalogue describes it. It holds every section of the product specification in order, each marked as compiled from facts, to be drafted, or left for your team. Actors and capabilities are listed with their tier, confidence and a citation id that resolves in `fact-index.md`; flows are summarized and Gap findings counted; business rules and acceptance criteria point to `rule-evidence.md` and `test_arrangements.md`. Sections an agent will draft say "Not drafted", since no agent writes a section yet. The header carries the catalogue version, export mode, graph content hash and curated revision. No model is involved, and every other exported file is unchanged.
