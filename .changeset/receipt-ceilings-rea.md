Raises the receipt ceilings so that a large TypeScript repository can cite every eligible fact. One extraction and the receipt store now keep up to 200,000 receipts and 128 MiB, up from 100,000 and 64 MiB, and a source can bind up to 200,000 facts. On the REA benchmark this keeps 155,264 receipts with no omitted declarations or rules, where it kept 54,725 before.
