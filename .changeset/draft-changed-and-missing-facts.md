Names the facts behind a stale section draft. For each accepted draft of a document section, `curated.md` now has a row under "Accepted section drafts" with its state and two lists: the cited facts whose content changed since the draft was written, and the cited ids that no longer resolve to a fact. The Spec Workbench read carries the same lists. They come from comparing recorded and current content hashes, with no model involved. The section appears only once a draft has been accepted.
