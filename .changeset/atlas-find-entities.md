Atlas has a Find entity field. It searches every entity in the loaded graph by display name, kind or stable ID, across all layers, focus levels and collapsed groups, and says so. Choosing a result opens its evidence and brings it into view: the layer widens to All layers only if needed, focus backs out only as far as needed, and the groups and cluster that hide it open. Clearing the search restores the usual indexes and keeps the current selection. Before, only the first 24 visible entities were listed, so an entity in a collapsed group or another layer could not be found.
