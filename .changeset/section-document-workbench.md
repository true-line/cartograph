Adds the document view to the Spec Workbench. Under "Sections and drafts", each section of the product specification is listed with its kind and every draft stored for it, with its claims, their T3 / InferredWeak annotation and their citation ids. "Draft this section" opens the investigation composer with the Section Writer role and the section's question filled in. Accept, Reject and Return record a review with an optional note at the revision on screen; a draft that changed since is refused. An accepted draft shows its state and reason from the curated read, and an accepted, current draft is printed in the next export.
