Every gap now says why it is missing and what would fill it. Each Gap is classified as an unsupported extractor, missing input, unresolved relationship, contradictory or stale observation, or genuine product or requirement gap. It also names the filling seam: an adapter, a registry entry, a manifest input, interpretation, or a requirement. The gap register adds a by-class rollup and per-row class columns, its JSON index records the same, and the Gaps lane shows the class on each gap and cause. Reasons no rule recognizes are labeled unclassified rather than guessed.
