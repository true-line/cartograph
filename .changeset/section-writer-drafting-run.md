Adds the Section Writer role (`section-writer@1`). It drafts one section of the product specification in an ordinary investigation run, under the same limits, consent and cancellation as the other roles. A run is started with a question that names the section and its scope (the whole system, or one actor, capability or rule); any other question is refused. When the run finishes with findings, each becomes one claim citing its facts by citation id, and the draft is stored for review. A run that finishes with insufficient evidence, proposes a design, or cites a fact outside the section's scope stores no draft. Review is not in the app yet, so exports still show "Not drafted".
