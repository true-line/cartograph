Shows what made a section draft stale in the Spec Workbench. An accepted draft that is stale or unresolvable now lists the cited facts that changed since it was accepted, each of which opens as evidence, and the cited ids that are no longer in the graph. Each claim that cites a changed fact says so. "Re-draft this section" on such a draft opens the investigation composer for the same section and scope; the accepted draft stays in place while the new one is written and reviewed.
