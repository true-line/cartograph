The Spec Workbench now shows the curated items. A **Curated items** section above the document list gives the curated revision (the one the bundle on screen records as `curated_basis`), the graph basis, the counts of accepted proposals by state, each curated item with its record id, proposed relation, Gap, tier and confidence as produced, review, source support and state, the accepted proposals that are not items grouped by state with the reason in a sentence, and the Workbench decisions that no longer match any statement. Every curated item is marked InferredWeak; in `verified-only` mode no item is shown, only how many were left out. When no proposal is accepted the section says so, and when the projection could not be computed it gives the reason and shows no proposal. The section is read again when you record a review, change the export mode, record a Workbench decision or choose **Refresh curated items**; recording a review now also recompiles the bundle, so `curated.md` in the Workbench no longer lags behind a review. Proposal history and the review dialog no longer say that an accepted proposal is "awaiting context reconciliation": they show the proposal's state and its reason when the read returned it, and otherwise point to the section.
