Shows what a re-draft changes. In the Spec Workbench, a draft that replaces another now has "Change from the draft it replaces": the claims added, removed and kept, and the cited facts that are new, no longer cited, or cited by both with different recorded content. It is there before you review the draft, and it comes from comparing the two stored drafts, with no model involved.
