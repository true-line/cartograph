You can now accept an investigation finding as curated in the app (ADR-0074). Each saved finding has **Accept as curated** and **Reject as curated** beside its follow-up review, with the saved decision, revision and time. The Spec Workbench lists the accepted findings in **Curated items**: the eligible ones with their statement and the facts they cite, and the others with the reason only. The Workbench reads the bundle again when a curation review is recorded.
