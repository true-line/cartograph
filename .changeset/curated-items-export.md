Every export now includes `curated.md`, and the bundle records a `curated_basis` (the curated revision and counts) beside `graph_basis`. The document lists every proposal you accepted. An accepted proposal whose evidence is all captured source text that is still readable, and whose graph and source associations are unchanged, is a curated item: a `best-effort` export shows it marked InferredWeak and cites its Gap, source and target; a `verified-only` export shows no item and states how many it left out. Every other accepted proposal is listed with one state (unverified, already-covered, unresolvable, stale or conflicted) and the reason in a sentence. Most accepted proposals are listed as unverified today, because their evidence was read from the working tree and nothing verified it. Accepting a proposal still writes nothing to the graph, resolves no Gap and leaves the graph hash and every other document unchanged. The document also summarises Workbench decisions, including those that no longer match any statement. An export reads at most 4,096 proposals and 256 accepted ones; beyond that, or when the proposal history cannot be read, the export still succeeds, `curated.md` says that the curated projection was not computed and why, and no accepted proposal is shown. The Workbench does not show curated items yet, and MCP readers and work packets cannot select the document.
